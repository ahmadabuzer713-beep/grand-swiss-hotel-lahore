About Us

Where Luxury Meets
Pakistani Hospitality

Nestled in the prestigious Johar Town neighborhood of Lahore, Grand Swiss Hotel & Apartments offers an unparalleled blend of Swiss precision and Pakistani warmth. Our meticulously designed spaces provide the perfect sanctuary for both business and leisure travelers.

Located just minutes from Expo Center Lahore and Emporium Mall, our prime location ensures you are never far from the city's finest attractions. Whether you are visiting for a corporate event, family vacation, or romantic getaway, we promise an experience that exceeds every expectation.
